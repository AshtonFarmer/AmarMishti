PROJECT CREATED BY:
Ashton Farmer

PROJECT TYPE:
Interactive cinematic birthday/love website

THEME:
Flash / Multiverse / Timelines / Love Across Universes

MAIN PURPOSE:
This website was handmade as a full emotional experience for
Tanima Das. The entire project is designed to feel like the
viewer is traveling through timelines and universes searching
for one specific person — and finding them every single time.

The website combines:
- cinematic transitions
- music
- video playback
- animated effects
- constellations
- emotional storytelling
- floating memories
- replayable universe loops

This is NOT just a webpage.
It is meant to feel like an experience.


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
                         MAIN FEATURES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

⚡ INTRO SCREEN
---------------------------------------------------------------
The first page opens with:
- "Happy Birthday Tanima"
- animated love atmosphere
- balloons
- lightning particles
- moving stars
- glowing effects
- Sheesha music in the background

A lightning bolt appears in the center.

When pressed:
- the phone vibrates slightly (mobile only)
- the music begins
- the multiverse transition starts


🌌 MULTIVERSE TRANSITION
---------------------------------------------------------------
The screen transitions into:
- moving constellations
- galaxy/star effects
- timeline visuals
- floating particles

Text appears:
"Searching the multiverse..."

After suspense:
"Tanima Das found ❤️"

This section is intentionally slowed down to create an
emotional reveal instead of instantly switching pages.


🎥 VIDEO PAGE
---------------------------------------------------------------
After the multiverse search:
the website enters the cinematic video page.

Features:
- fullscreen video
- glowing text
- emotional atmosphere
- smooth transition effects

The video represents:
finding each other across universes.


🌸 FLOWER ENDING
---------------------------------------------------------------
After the video:
flowers bloom upward from the bottom of the screen.

This symbolizes:
love taking over the universe/timeline.

Then:
"Ami tomake bhalobashi."
("I love you.")

appears as the emotional ending line.


🪐 MEMORY UNIVERSE
---------------------------------------------------------------
After the flowers:
the website enters the Memory Universe.

Photos appear floating around the screen like memories
from different universes and timelines.

Effects include:
- glowing photos
- drifting movement
- cinematic floating
- soft fades
- emotional timing

The memories intentionally feel:
dream-like
nostalgic
cosmic


📸 MEMORY ORDER
---------------------------------------------------------------
1. Purple saree bridge photo
2. Garden flower photos
3. Funny selfies
4. Restaurant memories
5. Hand holding moments
6. Jeep/carnival memories
7. Heart-hands carnival shot

The final memories are meant to emotionally feel like:
"Even across infinite universes...
I still find you."


🔁 REPLAY SYSTEM
---------------------------------------------------------------
At the end:
the replay button appears.

Replay does NOT simply refresh the page.

Instead:
it restarts the multiverse search,
making it feel like:
another universe beginning again.


😉 BEHIND THE SCENES BUTTON
---------------------------------------------------------------
The ending page includes:
"Behind The Scenes 😉"

When pressed:
the viewer sees the actual project code.

Purpose:
to show how much work, effort, and love went into creating
the experience completely by hand.
