AMAR MISHTI ❤️
A Multiverse Love Experience For Tanima Das

CREATED BY:
Ashton Farmer

PROJECT TYPE:
Interactive cinematic birthday website

THEME:
Love across timelines, universes, and lifetimes.

This project was handmade to feel less like a website and more like a living emotional experience.

It combines:
- cinematic storytelling
- multiverse concepts
- music
- memory sequences
- constellation visuals
- emotional transitions
- replayable universe loops
- floating timeline memories
- a car universe sequence
- hidden envelope jokes

The entire website follows one core idea:

"No matter the universe...
I would always find you again."


============================================================
FULL EXPERIENCE FLOW
============================================================

1. BIRTHDAY INTRO PAGE 🎂
------------------------------------------------------------
The experience begins on a birthday page for Tanima.

Features:
- balloons
- stars
- floating lightning particles
- glowing effects
- love atmosphere
- background music ("Sheesha")

A lightning bolt appears in the center of the page.

When pressed:
the journey through timelines begins.


2. CAR UNIVERSE SEQUENCE 🚗🌌
------------------------------------------------------------
Before the multiverse search, the website enters a car universe sequence.

Vehicles included:
- Orange Wrangler-style Jeep
- Red Bull / Oracle inspired Formula 1 car
- BMW-inspired sports car

Each car universe includes:
- animated driving roads
- moving stars
- glowing smoke text
- different cinematic lighting
- custom messages

Messages included:
- "Built different. Driven by love."
- "You'll always be my favorite destination."
- "My favorite road always leads to you."

This section is isolated in its own iframe system so the car animations do not break the main website styling.


3. MULTIVERSE SEARCH ⚡
------------------------------------------------------------
After the car sequence:

The screen transitions into:
- moving constellations
- glowing stars
- galaxy visuals
- multiverse scanning effects

Text appears:
"Searching the multiverse..."

Then scan logs appear one by one:
- Scanning timelines...
- Connection detected...
- Soul recognized...
- Synchronizing universes...

Finally:
"Tanima Das found ❤️"

appears as the emotional reveal.


4. CINEMATIC VIDEO PAGE 🎥
------------------------------------------------------------
After finding Tanima:
the website enters the cinematic video page.

The video contains:
- puzzle-piece symbolism
- connected memories
- emotional visuals
- fullscreen cinematic playback

The puzzle lines disappear at the end to symbolize:

"Nothing can break us."


5. ENVELOPE JOKES 💌
------------------------------------------------------------
Two envelopes appear beside the video.

When opened:
- they reveal hidden joke letters
- the envelope changes into an opened mail icon
- the opened state stays visible so she knows she already opened it

Included jokes:

Envelope 1:
"What did the chicken say to the pig?"
"Fawwwwkkkkk Off!"

Envelope 2:
"Why do seagulls fly over the ocean? Because if they flew over the bay, they'd be bagels."


6. FLOWER ENDING 🌸
------------------------------------------------------------
After the video:
flowers bloom upward from the bottom of the screen.

This represents:
love consuming the universe/timeline.

Then:
"Ami tomake bhalobashi."
("I love you.")

appears on screen.


7. ENDING PAGE ❤️
------------------------------------------------------------
After the flowers:
the ending page appears.

Buttons:
- Find us in another universe ⚡
- Behind the scenes 😉

The Behind The Scenes section reveals:
the actual source code of the website,
showing how much work and love went into it.


8. MEMORY UNIVERSE 🌌
------------------------------------------------------------
Clicking:
"Find us in another universe ⚡"

does NOT simply replay the website.

Instead:
it opens an entirely new universe.

Features:
- constellation transition
- memory scanning
- floating photographs
- drifting timeline fragments
- galaxy atmosphere
- emotional pacing

The floating memories represent:
different universes and lifetimes where two souls still found each other.


============================================================
MEMORY SEQUENCE
============================================================

The memory universe contains:
- cultural moments
- funny memories
- carnival/festival moments
- soft romantic photos
- candid smiles
- emotional memories

The photos intentionally float like:
timeline fragments drifting through space.

The purple saree bridge photo appears first to create a cinematic emotional opening.


============================================================
SUNSET HEARTBEAT RESTART ☀️
============================================================

At the end of the memory universe:

The entire universe begins collapsing.

Features:
- photos slowly dissolve away
- memories blur and drift upward
- stars collapse into darkness
- "Restarting fate..." appears

Then:
a detailed sunset orb forms in the center of the screen.

The orb contains:
- glowing sunset colors
- rotating light textures
- layered rings
- warm golden/pink/orange lighting
- heartbeat pulsing

The orb expands,
then shrinks into a tiny glowing heartbeat.

While the heartbeat pulses:
the birthday page slowly fades back in behind it.

The heartbeat finally disappears,
symbolizing:
the universe restarting itself once again.

The sunset colors were chosen because sunset tones are Tanima's favorite colors.
